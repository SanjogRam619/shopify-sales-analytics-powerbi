## 📌 Project Overview

The **Shopify Sales Analytics Dashboard** is an interactive Business Intelligence solution developed in **Microsoft Power BI** to provide meaningful insights into Shopify sales performance.

The dashboard enables users to monitor key business metrics, analyze revenue trends, evaluate product performance, compare regional sales, and explore payment gateway usage through interactive visualizations.

The project focuses on delivering a clean, executive-friendly interface with a modern dark theme and intuitive filtering experience.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

- Monitor overall sales performance.
- Track executive KPIs in real time.
- Analyze revenue trends.
- Identify top-performing states and cities.
- Compare revenue across product categories.
- Evaluate payment gateway contribution.
- Enable interactive business exploration using slicers.
